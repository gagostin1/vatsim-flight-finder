# Airport and route data

## Airport records

Airport names, municipalities, ICAO codes, latitudes, and longitudes were selected from the
[OurAirports `airports.csv` dataset](https://davidmegginson.github.io/ourairports-data/airports.csv)
on 2026-08-25. OurAirports releases all of its data to the public domain and provides it without
a guarantee of accuracy or fitness for use. Its terms and field definitions are available at:

- https://ourairports.com/data/
- https://ourairports.com/help/data-dictionary.html

The catalog intentionally includes only a small selection of eastern United States airports.

## Routes and estimates

Route pairs, center associations, and estimated durations were curated specifically for this
project. Durations are rounded gate-to-gate planning estimates for filtering recommendations;
they are not schedules, dispatch calculations, or navigation data. Actual flight time varies
with aircraft, routing, winds, traffic, and airport conditions.
